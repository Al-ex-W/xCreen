# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable. That includes their screen contents or notes.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Alexander Wahlström, through the contact details on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Al-ex-W). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content or block participants who violate this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
